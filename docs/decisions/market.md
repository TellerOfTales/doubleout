# Dominoes or darts? — a market sweep (September 2026)

Asked after the second playtest: "I'm wondering about changing to dominos if there's
no dominos game already dominating the market." Five research angles were run; the
demand-signals angle and the final synthesis died on a quota limit, so this is the
synthesis of the four that finished (mobile dominoes, PC/roguelike dominoes, the
darts market, the post-Balatro wave). Figures come from store-page and news snippets
rather than the pages themselves (the sandbox blocked the stores), so treat exact
numbers as medium confidence.

## Mobile dominoes: dominated AND crowded

- **Domino Dreams** (SuperPlay, bought by Playtika for $700M up front plus up to
  $1.25B earn-out, Nov 2024): $100M annualised run-rate by Aug 2024, Playtika's "#1
  game in the Board category", roughly a million downloads and $7M a month across
  both stores in early 2026. It is a level-based tile puzzle with a kingdom meta, not
  classic dominoes.
- Classic rulesets are a wall of 10M–100M-install ad-funded apps: Loop Games'
  *Dominoes* (100M+, 2.13M reviews), Domino Legends (24M), Jogatina (45M), Flyclops'
  *Domino!* (10M+), SNG's offline *Dominoes* (13M, still ~100K installs a month),
  plus regional giants (Higgs Domino 50M+). All Block/Draw/All Fives/Mexican Train,
  all ads + IAP + ad-free subscription, all with ad-fatigue complaints.
- The only empty pocket on mobile is a premium or roguelite dominoes game. Nothing of
  that shape surfaced.

## PC dominoes: nobody dominates — because the exact pitch has been tried five times

- No dominoes-first game on Steam has more than ~33 reviews. The "tiles on a table"
  fantasy belongs to Dorfromantik (15.7k reviews) and Carcassonne, not to pips.
- "Balatro with dominoes" shipped at least five times in fourteen months: *Delirium:
  Echoes of the Domino* (EA Jul 2025), *Dominova* (Nov 2025, the niche leader at
  26–33 reviews, 96%), *PUPAI* (Jan 2026, 2 reviews), *DOMISNAKE* (Jun 2026),
  *Dominocalypse* (13 Aug 2026, 10 reviews at 100%), with three more announced
  (*DOMiNi*, *Qumino*, *Dominoir*) and eight-plus itch prototypes. None found an
  audience. That is the strongest signal in the sweep: the niche is open because
  demand is soft, not because nobody thought of it.

## Darts, for comparison

- Mobile: less crowded and undominated. *Darts of Fury* (Yakuto, 10M+, 4.7, ~375K
  ratings) is the biggest, an order of magnitude below Loop Games' dominoes. Console
  darts is dormant (last licensed PDC game 2010, nothing announced despite the Luke
  Littler boom); VR is the only segment with publisher money.
- Roguelike/deckbuilder: sparse, but with recent entrants closer to this design than
  anything in dominoes. *Greedy Darts* (EA Oct 2025, cards and relics over 501 and
  Cricket) is commercially dead. *Dorts: A Darts Roguelike* (MovingPixels, 12 Aug
  2026, $8.99, first-person 501 with upgrades, 10–15 minute runs) has 22 reviews at
  100% and is the nearest loop match. *Pixel Darts: From Pub to Glory* (Jul 2026)
  matches the pixel-pub aesthetic with 10 reviews. Nothing above ~30 reviews either
  side.

## The post-Balatro map

Taken with a dominant hit: poker (Balatro), slots (CloverPit, Luck be a Landlord),
pachinko (Peglin, Ballionaire), blackjack (Dungeons & Degenerate Gamblers), mahjong
(Aotenjo and three others), chess, word games. Contested without a breakout: bingo,
roulette, pool, dice, bowling — and dominoes and darts, each with two to five
sub-scale entrants and no winner.

## Verdict

**Dominoes is the worse market of the two.** Its casual tier is owned by a
billion-dollar acquisition and a hundred-million-install incumbent, and its indie
tier has already absorbed the exact "Balatro with dominoes" pitch five times without
a hit. Darts has a real-world boom behind it, an undominated mobile tier, and a
roguelite lane whose entrants are tiny — the one to watch is *Dorts*, which is the
same 501 loop in first person and shipped three weeks ago.

What would change the answer: a dominoes design that is genuinely not "Balatro with
tiles" (the spatial/tile-matching puzzle, à la Domino Dreams, is where the money
actually is), or evidence that *Dorts* is breaking out.

The developer's read on seeing this: "sounds like dominos is oversaturated." The
work went into the darts loop instead — see DECISIONS.md #62–66.
